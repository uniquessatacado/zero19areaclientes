# Mockups do estúdio

Gerados com a ferramenta de imagem integrada em 18/09/2026, a partir dos atlas fotográficos locais. Não são fotografias do estoque real; servem como referência visual calibrável. As imagens geradas têm 1254 × 1254 px (cada vista é um quadrante). Exportar em resolução maior não cria detalhe fotográfico adicional.

## normal-offwhite

Arquivo: assets/shirt-normal-offwhite.png

Prompt:

Use case: product-mockup. Create a NEW photorealistic product photography sprite atlas for a professional T-shirt print-placement web studio, using supplied references only for photographic material/lighting style. Output highest resolution square 2x2 atlas with EXACT equal square quadrants, no gutter, no labels, no logo, NO PRINT. ALL four shirts are the SAME regular straight cut fitted classic short sleeve T-shirt in warm off-white ivory cotton, not bright white. Top-left quadrant: perfectly straight FRONT flat garment; top-right BACK flat garment. Bottom-left: side view prominently showing the WEARER LEFT outer sleeve, shirt points toward image left; bottom-right: mirrored side view showing WEARER RIGHT outer sleeve. In each front/back quadrant top shoulder at y5%, hem at y94%, torso sides x20.5% and79.5%, entire sleeves fit within x2%..98%. Side quadrants same framing as reference sleeves: visible outer sleeve face centered at x54.8% for left and45.2% for right, y31%, sleeve print face x43.5..66.5% (left), x33.5..56.5% (right), y15..47%. Stretch the garment pattern if needed to meet normalized bounds, preserving photorealism. Render gorgeous hyper-detailed cotton knit, collar and seam stitching, diffuse studio light, NO strong folds or wrinkles in any printing area, no cast shadows crossing printing area. Neutral very light gray background #e8e8e8, no mannequin visible. Same natural product photography style as references, not illustration, not CGI looking. These are blank mockups for compositing exact-size art. The oversized model MUST look noticeably boxier, dropped shoulder, sleeve wider/longer than regular. No typography, no annotation.
